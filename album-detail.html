Morat is a Colombian indie pop band formed in Bogotá in 2010, comprised of Juan Pablo Isaza, Juan Pablo Villamil, Martín Vargas, and Simón Vargas. Known for their emotive lyrics and acoustic sound, they became one of the most listened-to Latin groups of their generation. "Si Ayer Fuera Hoy" is their most recent album, a collection of songs that explore love, nostalgia, and the passage of time with their characteristic sensitivity.
